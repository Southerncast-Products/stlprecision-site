### Blog SEO Best Practices

1. **Keyword Optimization:**
   - Include primary keyword in H1 tag
   - Use keyword in first 100 words
   - Include related keywords naturally throughout
   - Target 1-2 primary keywords per article

2. **Content Structure:**
   - Use clear H2/H3 headings
   - Include 3-5 subheadings per 2,000 words
   - Add bullet points and numbered lists
   - Include relevant images with alt text

3. **Internal Linking:**
   - Link to related blog posts
   - Link to relevant service pages
   - Use descriptive anchor text
   - 3-5 internal links per article

4. **Meta Tags:**
   - Title: 50-60 characters, include primary keyword
   - Description: 150-160 characters, compelling CTA
   - Slug: lowercase, hyphens, descriptive

5. **Publishing:**
   - Publish 1-2 articles per month
   - Update older articles quarterly
   - Promote on social media
   - Build backlinks through industry partnerships
