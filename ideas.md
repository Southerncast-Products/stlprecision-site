# STL Precision Cast Products - Design Brainstorm

## Context
This is a redesign of an industrial foundry website for St. Louis Precision Cast Products. The company specializes in gray iron and ductile iron castings, is a MEEHANITE licensee, and has been serving American industry since 1970. The design should compete with premium foundry websites like Phillips Manufacturing and Howell Foundry.

## Idea 1: Industrial Brutalism

**Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalism meets Industrial Heritage

**Core Principles:**
1. Raw, unpolished aesthetic that celebrates the industrial process
2. Bold typography with stark contrasts
3. Exposed structural elements in the layout
4. Authentic imagery showcasing the craft

**Color Philosophy:**
- Primary: Deep Charcoal (#1C1C1C) - represents raw iron and steel
- Accent: Molten Orange (#FF6B35) - captures the heat of the foundry
- Secondary: Concrete Gray (#4A4A4A) - industrial authenticity
- Highlight: Safety Yellow (#FFD700) - industrial safety markers
- The palette evokes the foundry floor: dark, hot, powerful

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Asymmetric grid with intentional "broken" alignments
- Large negative spaces punctuated by bold content blocks
- Overlapping elements creating depth and tension
- Horizontal scrolling sections for product showcases

**Signature Elements:**
1. Diagonal cuts and angled section dividers mimicking metal shearing
2. Textured backgrounds with subtle grain/noise patterns
3. "Stamped" typography effects on headings

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Heavy, deliberate transitions (like moving metal)
- Hover states reveal hidden content (like uncovering a casting)
- Scroll-triggered reveals that feel like industrial machinery

**Animation:**
- Slow, weighted parallax scrolling
- Elements slide in from edges with momentum
- Text reveals character-by-character like a typewriter

**Typography System:**
- Display: Oswald Bold (industrial, condensed)
- Body: Source Sans Pro (clean, readable)
- Accents: Roboto Mono (technical specifications)

## Selected Approach: Idea 2 - Precision Engineering Elegance

I'm selecting the **Precision Engineering Elegance** approach because it:
1. Best reflects the company's core value proposition: PRECISION casting
2. Creates strong visual differentiation from competitors
3. Builds trust through clean, professional presentation
4. Allows for excellent SEO through clear content hierarchy
5. Maintains the Phillips Manufacturing-inspired aesthetic the user requested while adding unique character

### Implementation Notes:
- Use Roboto Condensed + Roboto font pairing
- Deep Navy (#1A365D) as primary with Foundry Red (#C41230) accents
- Clean white backgrounds with strategic use of light gray sections
- Card-based service presentation with hover animations
- Technical specification tables for materials
- Strong CTA sections with red buttons
- Sticky navigation with clean typography
